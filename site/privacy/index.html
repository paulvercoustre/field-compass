Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Sub-processors

Fieldcompass.org, an NGO based in Paris, France, is responsible for the personal data of the people who use Field Compass. It processes survey data only on behalf of the organisations that collect it.

Who we are

Fieldcompass.org, NGO, Paris, France. For anything about your data, write to privacy@fieldcompass.org.

Our two roles

  • For your account (people who use the app or visit fieldcompass.org), we are the controller.
  • For survey data that an organisation brings into Field Compass from KoboToolbox, we are a processor. We act only on that organisation's instructions, under a data processing agreement. The organisation is the controller.

If you were interviewed for a survey, please contact the organisation that interviewed you. If you write to us, we will pass your request to them.

What we process for organisations

When an organisation connects a KoboToolbox project, Field Compass stores:

  • the form and the submissions, with their edit history;
  • the time spent on each question, from Kobo's audit logs;
  • transcripts of recorded answers, translations, and the findings of the quality checks, including AI review;
  • review decisions and reviewer notes.

Recordings are read from Kobo when someone plays them or when they are transcribed, and are not kept. All of this is deleted when the organisation deletes the survey, or when its owner deletes their account. Copies in backups are gone within 97 days: weekly copies are kept 90 days, then 7 more as deleted copies. What Field Compass wrote back to Kobo (review decisions, transcripts, translations) stays in Kobo.

Visitors to fieldcompass.org

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies. It loads no fonts or scripts from other services.

Who receives data

Only our sub-processors, listed below with where they process data, and only to provide the service. We never sell data or use it for advertising. We do not use account or survey data to train AI models, and neither do our AI sub-processors.

Transfers outside the EU

Field Compass is hosted in the EU: Microsoft Azure in Ireland, with backups in the Netherlands.

When a survey uses AI review, translation or transcription on the allowance Field Compass provides, the answers or recordings concerned are sent to OpenAI or ElevenLabs. They may process them in the United States, under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. A survey's owner can connect their own AI provider instead, or leave these features off.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to limit its use, or object to uses based on our legitimate interest. You can delete your account yourself in Account settings; this also deletes the surveys you own and all their data.

Write to privacy@fieldcompass.org. We answer within one month. You can also complain to the CNIL: 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, cnil.fr.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored keys are encrypted again by Field Compass, and access is limited by role. The security annex to our data processing agreement lists the measures.

Changes

We will post changes here and tell account holders about important ones in the app. Last updated: 18 October 2026.
